Agents as vendors

"Managing a complex AI agentic bot system is sort of like managing a contract with different vendors." The site owner

A vendor does work you cannot watch all day. You do not read every line it writes. You write down what you want, check what comes back, and decide what happens when it goes wrong. This setup does the same with agents. Each row below names a vendor-management practice and the page where this setup does it.

The map

Where the analogy is thin

  • A human vendor has its own standards. An agent has only the rules in its brief and the rules file.
  • A human vendor can refuse. By default, workers here skip permission checks, so the brief and the review carry the control. A model-reviewed mode is being trialled. See Cast and authority.
  • The staff-bot side of the setup, the part closest to a vendor roster, is designed and has not run. See Task management.

In short. Scope the work, write down who may do what, make workers report, accept work only on evidence, and name what happens when something fails. These are vendor-management habits, applied to agents.
