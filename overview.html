What is this?

This is a tour of a personal setup for running AI coding agents. The idea in one sentence: keep your intent and the agents' actions as plain files, compare the two, and approve every change to your rules.

The supervisor and most crew mechanics are upstream Firstmate, MIT licence, by Kun Chen and contributors. These pages link to its docs and do not copy them. The owner built the other parts: the shared rules file and rooms, the hooks, the work index and the review flow. Shell tests check them.
