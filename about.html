I'm Ashish Raina. I run a crew of AI coding agents from one chat, and this site, The Chart Room, shows how: how I steer them, how I check their work, and how I keep them doing what I meant.

"Managing a complex AI agentic bot system is sort of like managing a contract with different vendors."

That is how I work. Each agent is a vendor with a brief, a scope and a deliverable. I review the work, I approve what ships, and I write the rules down so the next one starts from them. Agents as vendors says more.

Where to start:

  • Field Notes: posts on harness engineering and alignment.
  • The harness arena: eleven agent setups on the same twelve parts, and my own radar.
  • Safety by design: what the agents may do alone, what waits for me, and what they never do.
  • Chat with EA-bot: the same story, one click at a time.

Most of the machinery is Kun Chen's open-source FirstMate stack. Each page credits who made what.
