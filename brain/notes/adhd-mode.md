# ADHD mode

Claim: ADHD mode puts the key point first, in bold, then gives one idea per line, short lists and one clear next step.

Evidence: The rules are a reading layout for people who scan: the answer is in the first line, nothing important hides in a long paragraph, and the reply ends with what to do now.

Elaboration:
- Key point first and bold. If the reader stops after one line, they still have the answer.
- One idea per line. Short lists, not long paragraphs.
- End with one clear next step.
- It is a writing style, not a medical tool.
- It is one of three talk modes, with [[caveman-mode]] and [[ste100-mode]]. Each mode is a small skill: a short description is always loaded, and the full rules load only when the mode is picked ([[progressive-disclosure]]).
