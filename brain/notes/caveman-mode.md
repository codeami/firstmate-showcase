# Caveman mode

Claim: Caveman mode answers in as few words as possible, with no filler, no greetings and no repeats of the question.

Evidence: The rule is to keep only the words that carry meaning. Fewer words also means fewer tokens to read and pay for.

Elaboration:
- Very short. Drop small words when the meaning stays clear.
- No filler: no "great question", no summary of what was just said.
- Facts, numbers and names stay exact. Only the padding goes.
- It is one of three talk modes, with [[adhd-mode]] and [[ste100-mode]]. Each mode is a small skill that loads only when picked ([[progressive-disclosure]]).
